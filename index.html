Before & after

Same Grok. Better question. Way better answer.

This is the kind of upgrade we practice in class. Pick a task and compare.

✗ Weak prompt

"Write a Facebook post for my restaurant."

Sample answer

Come visit our restaurant! We have great food and friendly service. Stop by today!

✓ Strong prompt

"I own a lakeside burger spot at Lake of the Ozarks with a boat dock. Write a Facebook post for this Friday's live music night, 6 to 9 PM. Warm, local, a little fun. Under 60 words, one emoji max. End with: Dock or drive, we'll save you a seat."

Sample answer

Friday night on the water 🎶 Live music from 6 to 9, smash burgers off the grill, and the best sunset seats at the Lake. Pull up to the dock or park out front, bring the crew and stay a while. Dock or drive, we'll save you a seat.

✗ Weak prompt

"Reply to this bad review."

Sample answer

We're sorry you had a bad experience. We strive for excellence in everything we do. Please contact us.

✓ Strong prompt

"I run a boat rental at the Lake. A customer left 2 stars: their boat was 30 minutes late and nobody called. Write a reply that owns the mistake with no excuses, says we now text when a boat is running late, and offers 20% off next time. Under 70 words. Sign it Chris, owner."

Sample answer

You're right, and I'm sorry. Waiting 30 minutes with no call is no way to start a lake day. We now text every customer the moment a boat is running behind. I'd love a chance to make it right: 20% off your next rental, just mention this review. Chris, owner

✗ Weak prompt

"Make me a plan for the week."

Sample answer

Monday: plan your week. Tuesday: work on important tasks. Wednesday: check your progress. Thursday: keep going. Friday: review the week.

✓ Strong prompt

"I'm a realtor at the Lake. This week: showings Tuesday and Thursday afternoon, a closing Friday at 10, and I want 3 Facebook posts. I work 8 to 5 and keep Wednesday mornings for family. Give me a simple day-by-day plan with my top 3 priorities each day."

Sample answer
  • Mon: confirm Tuesday's showing, draft all 3 posts, follow up with 5 leads
  • Tue: post #1, prep and run the showing, send the buyer a recap
  • Wed (from noon): client calls, post #2, order a closing gift
  • Thu: showing, final walkthrough check, send docs to title
  • Fri: closing at 10, post #3 celebrating it, plan next week

Sample answers, written to show the difference a better prompt makes. Real Grok answers will vary.
